# ProAudioStar — in-person visit list

**Date:** 2026-08-18 · **Store:** ProAudioStar, Brooklyn NY
**Verified:** all 15 listings re-checked live on Reverb the morning of the visit; every price unchanged.

---

## The one thing that decides this trip

**Not one of these 15 listings publishes a weight.** That is 30% of the score sitting blank on
every single guitar in the building, and it is the criterion you have already proved you can feel —
the black LP Custom at 30th Street was right on everything except weight, and that killed it.

So the trip has one job: **put a scale under everything you like.** Ask them to weigh it, or bring a
luggage scale. Your ceilings are LP ≤ 9.0 lbs (ideal 8.3–8.8) and ES ≤ 8.0 lbs.

Second job: **the necks.** Almost nothing here has measured depths either. Bring the calipers if you
have them; if not, the 1999 Dot's neck is your reference point — you liked it, but less than the
1-9/16" necks. Anything that beats the Dot is worth a hard look.

Everything else — pickups, wiring, tuners — is reversible for a couple hundred dollars.
Weight and neck carve are not. On a forever guitar, rank by weight and feel first and let the
pickups sort themselves out later.

## Play order

### 1. g404 — Custom Shop 1959 Reissue, CME Spec · $5,200 · score 74.9
Cherry Tea Burst · Gibson Custom "S" Buckers · 2-piece figured top · OHSC + COA
**https://reverb.com/item/94932822**

**The find of this visit.** The Reverb spec block names the neck: **'60 V2** — one of Gibson Custom's
documented carves (~.830" at the 1st, ~.940" at the 12th), the medium-slim one sitting between the
'59 club and the V3 skinny. This is the same family of answer as the Carmelita CME listing you sent:
a named, replicated shape rather than a depth number a dealer guessed at.

That single line moved it from 53.0 to 74.9 and made it the highest-scoring guitar at the shop.

Watch for: it is the only guitar here not priced under market (5% under anchor), and "Very Good"
condition at $5,200 wants justifying. Offers are on, 152 days listed — $4,450 is a fair opening number.

### 2. g402 — Custom Shop 1960 Reissue R0, 2015 · $4,200 · score 70.0
Dark Burst · Custombucker Alnico III · **figured** top · 60s SlimTaper · OHSC + COA
**https://reverb.com/item/90905732**

R0 is your top LP model and the SlimTaper is the carve family you want. Figured top, and the
description confirms Custombuckers — same pickups as g403 below, so the two R0s differ on top figure,
year and price, not electronics.

**Listed 418 days.** That is by far the softest price in the building; 39% under anchor already and
the seller has been sitting on it well over a year. $3,550 is a realistic opening offer.

### 3. g403 — Custom Shop 1960 Reissue R0, 2007 · $3,900 · score 70.0
Dark Burst · Custombucker Alnico III · **plain** top · 60s SlimTaper · OHSC + COA
**https://reverb.com/item/94932825**

Same guitar as g402 in every way that affects how it plays, $300 cheaper, eight years older, plain

### 11. g408 — 2024 Standard '60s Faded, Bourbon Burst · $2,020 · **currently excluded**

detour even though the guitar is not.

## Ask at the counter

1. **Weigh every guitar you play.** Nothing here publishes a weight.
2. **g404:** confirm the '60 V2 neck on the COA, and ask what the CME spec run's carve actually was —
   whether it is the same neck family CME described as fast-feeling because of the slimmer shoulders.
3. **g406:** get the full story on the repaired crack near the nut. Who did it, when, is it stable.
4. **g402 vs g403:** ask which has been on the floor and which came out of a box — 418 days listed
   usually means a guitar nobody plays, and the frets will show it either way.
5. **Cases:** g407 and g385 ship without hard cases. Ask if one can be included in a deal.
6. **In-store price vs Reverb price.** These are Reverb numbers. Ask whether the floor price differs
   before you anchor on them.

## One caveat about the list

Every listing above is located Brooklyn, NY, but ProAudioStar's Reverb shop record resolves to a
Texas address, and a shop-level inventory query returned none of these guitars. So this list is built
from listing-level data, not from a confirmed Brooklyn floor manifest. **Floor stock may differ from
what is listed online** — the same thing happened at 30th Street, where the black LP Custom you played
appears nowhere on their site. Call ahead if any single guitar is the reason you are going.
