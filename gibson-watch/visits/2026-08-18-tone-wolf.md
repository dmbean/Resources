**Store:** Tone Wolf, Brooklyn NY (Reverb shop `tone-wolf`, id 2675473 — address confirmed Brooklyn,
unlike ProAudioStar)
**Swept:** 2026-08-18, full shop inventory — 497 listings, 21 relevant, 11 of them new to the database.

---

## The headline

**Tone Wolf publishes the weight and the 1st/12th-fret neck depths on every single guitar.** That is
the exact opposite of ProAudioStar, where none of the 19 listings published a weight. So this list is
ranked on real measurements rather than on guesses, and you can decide most of it before you walk in.

Which produces a blunt finding:

> **Exactly one Gibson Les Paul in the shop is inside your weight band — and it costs $18,499.**

Everything else Gibson-branded is at the ceiling or over it. The good news is that the one guitar
that lands well is also cheap, and it lands *very* well.

**And there is no Gibson ES-335 at Tone Wolf at all.** Not one, in 497 listings. This is a Les Paul
trip. Details in the semi-hollow section below.

## A pattern worth knowing before you go

You played a **black Les Paul Custom** at 30th Street, liked it a lot, and passed on the weight.
Tone Wolf has two more black Customs — **9 lbs 11 oz** and **9 lbs 15 oz**. That is now three
Customs in a row over your ceiling.

That is not coincidence. Les Paul Customs carry an ebony board, multi-ply binding on body, neck and
headstock, and usually a maple top, and they were rarely weight-relieved in the eras these come from.
**If the Custom is the guitar you want, the weight fight is structural, not bad luck** — you would be
hunting for an unusually light example rather than an average one, and at 9.0 lbs that hunt is long.
Worth deciding now whether you want a Custom badly enough to wait for it.

## Ask at the counter

1. **g389 (the R0):** it is graded "Good" — get them to walk you through every mark. Also ask whether
   the unpotted Custombuckers squeal at volume; unpotted is what you asked for, but it has a cost.
2. **g389 again:** 444 days listed. Ask directly what it would take to move it today.
3. **g863 (Pre-Historic):** ask them to weigh it in front of you. One ounce is the whole question.
4. **g862 (CC#1):** just ask to play it. It is an $18k guitar; expect to be supervised, and be
   straightforward that you are not buying it today.
5. **Anything not on Reverb.** Tone Wolf's shop address checks out as Brooklyn, so the online list
   should broadly match the floor — but ask what came in this week and has not been photographed yet.
   That is where the 1-9/16″-nut 335 you actually want will surface.
