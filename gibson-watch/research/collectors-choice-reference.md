# Gibson Custom Shop "Collector's Choice" Les Pauls — Reference Table

Compiled 2026-08-13 for a buyer prioritising a **slim, late-1960 "Skinny C" carve (~.80" 1st fret / ~.89" 12th)** at 8.3–8.8 lbs under $10k.

## How to read this

The Collector's Choice (CC) series ran **2010–2017**. Each model is a replica of one specific, named vintage guitar, and Gibson copied that donor guitar's neck contour. **The carve therefore belongs to the model, not to the year** — this is the single most important fact for this buyer, because several 1960-donor CCs have fat '59-style necks and at least one 1959-donor CC is on the slim side.

Numbers below are **real measurements published by dealers on individual instruments** (Chicago Music Exchange, The Music Zoo, Carter Vintage, Willcutt) or by Gibson's own spec sheets. Because they are per-instrument, expect ±.01–.02" of unit-to-unit variance; where several units of one model were measured, the observed spread is shown.

**Carve bands used here**
| Band | 1st-fret depth | Character |
|---|---|---|
| **SLIM** | ~.78–.85" | late-1960 "Skinny C" / V3 — the buyer's target |
| **MEDIUM** | ~.85–.89" | transitional '58/'59-ish |
| **FAT / CHUNKY** | ~.89–.95"+ | classic '58–'59 baseball-bat |

`UNKNOWN` means no real source published a 1st-fret figure. **UNKNOWN is never a guess** — the general "1960 = slimmer" pattern is noted but deliberately not applied as data.

Numbers that **do not exist**: CC20, CC21, CC23, CC25, CC27, CC32, CC40, CC41.

## Production and pricing

- **Run sizes:** most planned at 300 units, frequently under-built. Smallest run: **CC42 (25)**. Others notably short: CC1A (125), CC9 (100), CC34 (100), CC35 (100), CC4V (50).
- **Aged + VOS pairs** were issued for CC1, CC4, CC29 only. All others were aged to varying degrees.
- **Original MSRP** ranged roughly $5,332 (CC19) to $13,645 (CC38); most bursts landed $6,300–$10,500.
- **Used market today (2026), from live and recently-sold US dealer asks:**
  - Common bursts (CC7, CC13, CC15, CC16, CC24, CC26, CC28, CC30, CC35, CC44, CC45, CC46): **$7,000–$10,000**
  - Premium / short-run (CC33, CC37, CC38, CC43): **$10,500–$12,500**
  - CC1 (either version) and CC2 are the outliers: **$14,000–$22,000**
  - CC10 Tom Scholz and CC12 Henry Goldtop are the soft end: **$3,500–$4,000**
