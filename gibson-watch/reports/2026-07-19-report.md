## Status: BLOCKED — no listings ingested (environment network policy)

This was the bootstrap run. Six parallel research agents swept the full dealer roster
(Wildwood, Willcutt, CME, Dave's, Reverb, Music Zoo, Emerald City, Carter, Gruhn, Elderly,
Cream City, Music Emporium, Southside, Rudy's, TR Crandall, Rainbow, Empire, GC Used,
Music Go Round), but **every outbound page fetch was denied with HTTP 403 by the
environment's egress proxy** — including control fetches to wikipedia.org and example.com.
Only server-side WebSearch worked.

Because the database rules require (a) verifying each individual product detail page as live
and available, and (b) exact weights and neck measurements taken from that page, zero
listings could be verified for ingestion. Rather than pollute the database with stale or
spec-less entries, the run recorded ~45 unverified candidate URLs in
`candidates-unverified.md` as a ready-made fetch list for the next run.

## Fix required

Change this environment's network policy to allow outbound HTTPS (at minimum: reverb.com,
the dealer domains in `dealers.md`, reddit.com, thegearpage.net, mylespaul.com), or move the
daily trigger to an environment with unrestricted egress. See
https://code.claude.com/docs/en/claude-code-on-the-web for environment network policies.
